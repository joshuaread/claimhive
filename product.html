DEEP CAPABILITIES

Six hubs. Complete mastery.

Each hub operates with specialized autonomy, exchanging live claim context so you never enter the same date, number, or carrier detail twice.

HUB 01

Claim CRM

PIPELINE TO SETTLEMENT

The central operational spine for every active file. Tracks every milestone from initial property intake to settlement check clearance, replacing fragmented spreadsheets with linear momentum.

Live Claim Map

Visualize your entire firm's docket across storm paths, counties, and carrier clusters. Spot geographic trends and deploy field adjusters with zero wasted windshield time.

State Statute Compliance

Pre-loaded with statutory deadlines across all 50 states (e.g. Florida 14-day acknowledgement rules, Texas prompt payment laws). Never miss a statutory cutoff.

Stale Claims Tracker

Flags stalled carrier responses immediately. Automatically computes days without carrier communication and drafts escalation notices.

Claim Strategy Wizard

Surfaces the next tactical move for each file—whether invoking appraisal, submitting an Ordinance or Law claim, or demanding an umpire inspection.

HUB 02

Weather Intel + Roofs

FORENSIC PROOF & GEOMETRY

Unassailable weather evidence attached to the file before carrier adjusters arrive on site.

Multi-Source Hail Validation

Cross-references NOAA Doppler radar, certified ground spotters, and atmospheric swath records to definitively prove hail stone diameter and date of loss.

Single-Click Roof Measurements

Generates high-resolution 3D aerial roof geometry, square calculations, eave/rake lengths, and pitch profiles in seconds without ladder risk.

AI-Assisted Lead Generation

Identifies heavily impacted commercial and residential structures along storm corridors to inform client outreach and community assistance.

HUB 03

Chip + 54 AI Agents

PRIVATELY HARNESSED LLM

Chip coordinates a swarm of 54 specialized AI agents operating on an isolated, privately harnessed large language model. Legally guaranteed zero training on your files.

Instant Policy Extraction

Parses 80-page carrier policies in seconds. Extracts Coverage A, B, C, D limits, hurricane deductibles, endorsement caps, and flags cosmetic exclusions.

Estimate Comparison & Omissions

Scans carrier Xactimate/Symbility sheets line by line against PA targets. Flags omitted starter courses, drip edges, continuous gutters, and denied O&P.

Demand Letters & Correspondence

Drafts certified demand letters with statutory state code citations, carrier stall notices, and formal proof-of-loss packets ready for licensed PA review.

Email, Check & Attachment Ingestion

Forward any email into the claim inbox. Chip extracts PDF estimates, updates adjuster contact info, logs settlement checks, and updates the docket automatically.

AI Field Inspections (Voice & Photo)

Speak your findings into the mobile mic while walking the roof. Chip transcribes room observations, sorts test square photos, and matches moisture meter readings.

Automated Intake

Drop a representation agreement or dec page into the intake portal. Chip populates the entire file, initiates deadline clocks, and organizes document folders.

HUB 04

Client Portal

PASSWORDLESS MAGIC LINKS

Give property owners real-time, transparent progress without fielding 10+ status calls every week. Free forever for clients and external experts.

Visual Activity Tracker

A clean milestone timeline showing insureds what has been accomplished, upcoming inspections, and next expected dates.

Voice & Text Claim Updates

Send 30-second audio briefs or automated SMS notices when a supplement is filed or an appraisal check is issued.

Document Upload & E-Signatures

Clients upload repair invoices and sign retainers or proof-of-loss affidavits directly from their smartphone without login friction.

HUB 05

Growth Hub

FIRM EXPANSION & REPUTATION

Scale your practice with automated reputation marketing, referral partner portals, and real-time business development analytics.

Blog Generator & Social Integration

Turn recent settlements into anonymized case studies, state insurance educational posts, and social media announcements.

Google Review Wizard

Automatically triggers 5-star review requests at the exact moment of peak client happiness—when the settlement check arrives.

BD Portal & Sales Analytics

Track which roofing contractors, property managers, and attorneys send you the most profitable property claims.

Back Office

FINANCIAL CLARITY & COMPLIANCE

Revenue Tracker (Owed at Every Stage)

Virtual CFO
