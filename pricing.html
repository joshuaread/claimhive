Questions, answered.

What counts as token usage?

Tokens measure the AI work ClaimHive does for you — Chip drafting letters, summarizing documents, researching storms. Light users use few; power users use more. Currently tracking at fractions of a cent per operation.

Will I see my usage?

Yes. Usage is shown by seat and by feature inside ClaimHive, so the bill is never a surprise.

What do beta testers pay?

Founding members locked into the November 1 Beta cohort get a lifetime rate of $80/seat (our planned GA rate is subject to change), and a starting balance of complimentary tokens.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We operate month-to-month. If ClaimHive isn't saving you time and drastically increasing your caseload capacity, you can export your data and leave at any time.

How does enterprise pricing work?

Firms with more than 10 users can negotiate pricing built around team size and expected usage. Talk to us and we will build it with you.
