Questions, answered.

What counts as token usage?

Tokens measure the compute work ClaimHive's AI does for your firm—Chip drafting demands, reading carrier policies, parsing checks, and sorting storm photos. Light users use few; power users pay only for the volume they generate. Costs are fractions of a cent per action.

Will I see my usage clearly?

Yes. Live usage telemetry is visible in your settings by seat and by tool. You can set spend alerts or hard caps per adjuster so there is zero ambiguity.

Who gets the founding rate?

Any firm that joins before January 31, 2027 locks the founding rate of $80/seat per month through June 30, 2027. Firms in the November 1 Beta also get a starting balance of complimentary AI tokens.

Is there an annual contract?

No. Billing is month to month. You can cancel at any time and export all your data.

How does enterprise pricing work?

Firms with 11 or more seats receive custom pooled token allowances, dedicated data migration specialists, and custom integrations tailored to your caseload.
