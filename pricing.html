PRICING CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after June 30, 2027?
Founding Members who join during our Alpha and January cohorts will retain their preferred pricing terms. Even if our public list price increases for new firms in 2027, you will receive our guaranteed grandfathered rates and first access to new capabilities.
Can I add or remove seats during storm season?
Yes. You can add temporary seats when a hurricane or severe freeze hits, and scale them down when the storm docket settles. You are only billed for active seats on a month-to-month basis with zero penalties.
Why don't you charge a percentage of claim settlements?
Because that contingent fee belongs to you. You climbed the steep roof, negotiated with hostile carrier adjusters, and spent hours preparing the estimate. Software is a utility, not a partner in your business. We charge a fair flat fee for the tool and let you keep 100% of your earnings.
Do policyholders have to pay or install an app?
Never. Your policyholder receives a secure, SMS-verified web link that works on any smartphone or browser. They can view real-time claim status, see inspection photos, and upload receipts without paying a dime or remembering a password.
How does onboarding work? Is there a setup fee?
Zero setup fees. When you are admitted to Claim Hive, Tom and our team walk you through your first three claims on a private 1-on-1 screen share. We configure your state's statutory rules, import your existing open claims, and get you moving in under an hour.
How hard is it to switch from our current software or spreadsheets?
Most adjusters avoid switching because legacy systems take weeks to set up. With Claim Hive, there's zero downtime. You don't have to migrate your entire 10-year historical archive on day one. You can import your current active files in under 30 minutes, or simply run your next three new claims in Claim Hive. Tom personally guides your setup on a private screen share so you never drop a deadline.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major corporate credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and ACH bank transfers for firms with 5 or more seats. Invoices and receipts are automatically generated each month for your accounting records.
