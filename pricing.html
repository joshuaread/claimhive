CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does token usage work?

Token usage covers the exact raw computing power when Chip reads an 80-page policy PDF, compares line-item estimates, or drafts a complex statutory demand letter. Tokens are passed through transparently at direct wholesale rates. An average claim costs just a few dollars in token usage over its entire lifecycle.

What are the terms for Beta access and early cohorts?

Private Alpha is underway with founding firms through October. Public Beta opens November 1, 2026. Beta firms receive personal, direct onboarding with our engineering team, early access to new agent releases, and a permanent rate lock on their base seat price through General Availability (GA) on January 1, 2027.

Am I locked into an annual contract?

No. ClaimHive operates on straightforward month-to-month billing. You can export your full data archive in a standard ZIP file at any time with a single click.

Do you charge per-claim fees or take a fee split?

Never. We charge $80 per seat plus token usage. Whether your firm settles a $30,000 residential water claim or a $5,000,000 commercial loss, we never take a percentage or charge a per-file fee.
