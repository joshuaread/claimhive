THE 5 TENETS OF DATA CUSTODY

Engineered for Insurance Regulatory Compliance

TENET 02

US-Based SOC-2 Data Vault

All claim repositories, inspection photographs, and carrier communications are hosted in SOC-2 Type II certified domestic US data facilities (AWS GovCloud / US-East & US-Central).

  • • AES-256 Encryption at Rest: Every PDF, photo, and database row is encrypted with dedicated KMS keys.
  • • TLS 1.3 in Transit: Perfect forward secrecy prevents interception across mobile and office networks.
  • • Geographically Redundant: Continuous replication guarantees disaster recovery during hurricanes.
US-only data sovereignty • Zero offshore routing

TENET 03

1-Click Full Data Export

We never hold your files hostage. At any moment, an administrator can generate a complete export of your entire firm's caseload with a single click.

  • • Standard Open Formats: High-resolution original JPEG/PNG photos, standard PDF files, and clean Excel/CSV chronologies.
  • • Instant ZIP Generation: No 30-day "support ticket wait times" or extortionate export fees.
  • • Total Ownership: You own the data. Period.
Zero proprietary lock-in • Complete portability

TENET 04

Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation

Every public adjusting firm operates within an isolated cryptographic partition. There is zero risk of an adjuster at Firm A accidentally seeing a file from Firm B.

  • • Granular Staff Roles: Limit access between senior PAs, field apprentices, estimators, and front-desk staff.
  • • Expiring Guest Tokens: Outside structural engineers and forensic CPAs only access the specific scope items you approve.
  • • Immutable Audit Logs: Every access timestamp is recorded to prove chain-of-custody in court.
Role-based access control (RBAC) • Cryptographic boundaries
