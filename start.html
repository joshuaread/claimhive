The short answer.

ClaimHive is the AI command center built with working public adjusters. It reads the claim emails in your connected Gmail and drafts the reply for you to approve. Each claim keeps its documents, tasks, deadlines and timeline in one file, and state deadlines are tracked on the claim for you to confirm. It also has a leads list with a lead form for your website, and a firm admin view with a CFO view, commissions and carrier check tracking. Agents that work one claim in parallel are coming before GA.

    FAQ

    What software do public adjusters use to manage claims?

    Public adjusters often spread a claim across email, folders, spreadsheets and notes. ClaimHive is claim management built for that work. It drafts replies to your claim emails and keeps each claim's documents, tasks, deadlines and timeline in one file.

    Is ClaimHive a CRM for public adjusters?

    ClaimHive is claim management for public adjusters, with a leads list and a lead form for your website. Each claim has its documents, tasks, deadlines and timeline in one file.

    Does ClaimHive send emails for me?

    No. ClaimHive drafts the reply. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

    Which email does ClaimHive work with?

    Your Gmail. You connect it during setup.

    How does ClaimHive track state deadlines?

    ClaimHive tracks state deadlines on the claim, including proof of loss. You confirm the dates for your states.

    Can I get leads from my own website?

    Yes. ClaimHive has a lead form you can put on your own website, and a leads list with AI review.

    Can I use my own templates for letters and documents?

    Yes. ClaimHive builds letters and documents from your own templates.

    Can I track carrier checks and commissions?

    Yes, for admin roles. ClaimHive has a CFO view, commissions and carrier check tracking.

    How much does ClaimHive cost?

    Free until January 1, 2027. Then $80/seat/month, the founding rate. We estimate AI usage at about $50 per seat per month for a typical seat. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

    What happens to my client files?

    ClaimHive does not use customer claim documents, estimates, photos, or messages to train public AI models or models shared across firms. The details are in the section below.

    What does the claim team preview show?

    A preview of specialist agents working one claim at the same time, with you approving what leaves. It is coming before GA and is not in the beta today.

    What happens to your client files.

    ClaimHive does not use customer claim documents, estimates, photos, or messages to train public AI models or models shared across firms.

    • When ClaimHive's AI sorts email, reads a document or drafts a reply, the content, including email content, goes to a third-party AI provider. The provider processes it under its own terms, and we do not make a retention guarantee on its behalf.

    • Google OAuth tokens encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
