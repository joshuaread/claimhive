LEGAL & DATA SOVEREIGNTY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026 • Effective immediately for all ClaimHive accounts.

1. Executive Plain-English Summary

The Bottom Line: ClaimHive Inc. ("ClaimHive", "we", "our") is designed specifically for licensed USA Public Adjusters. We do not sell your personal information, monetize your client lists, broker leads to third parties, or train public AI foundation models on your claim documentation. Your files belong exclusively to your firm and your clients.

We operate under the fundamental understanding that property claim documentation—including fee agreements, proof of loss filings, estimates, expert causation reports, and carrier correspondence—constitutes sensitive, often legally privileged work product. This policy explains clearly and without obscure legal jargon how we handle and protect that information.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide and secure the ClaimHive operating system:

  • Account & Adjuster Credentials: Full name, firm name, state adjuster license number(s), work email address, phone number, and seat allocation counts.
  • Billing & Payment Information: Payment processing is handled directly via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors (Stripe). We do not store raw credit card numbers or bank account passwords on our servers.
  • Claim Work Product (Uploaded by You): Insurance policies, declaration pages, scope notes, inspection photos, repair estimates, statutory notices, and client contact details entered into your secure tenant vault.
  • Operational Audit Logs: Cryptographic access records, login timestamps, IP addresses, and document action events required to maintain chain of custody for legal and regulatory compliance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to power your firm’s operations:

  • Executing file docketing, automatic policy limits extraction, and photo sorting.
  • Calculating and monitoring state-specific statutory deadlines (e.g., 60-day Proof of Loss statutory clocks).
  • Generating pre-drafted carrier letters and scheduling the daily Next Action morning docket.
  • Providing policyholders with a private, read-only status view of their ongoing claim.
  • Diagnosing software bugs, preventing malicious access, and verifying system uptime.

What we will NEVER do: We will never sell, lease, or distribute your claim files, policyholder contact info, or estimate line items to insurance carriers, lead brokers, roofing contractors, or marketing aggregators.

4. Zero AI Model Training Guarantee

Privileged Work Product Clause: ClaimHive formally covenants that customer claim documents, estimates, photos, and messages will NOT be used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or calibrate public or proprietary commercial artificial intelligence foundation models.

When document extraction features are triggered (e.g., reading coverage limits from a declarations page), processing occurs through isolated inference pipelines covered by strict enterprise zero-data-retention (ZDR) agreements. Once extraction finishes, the temporary memory buffer is destroyed. Your claim data never leaks across tenant boundaries.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Infrastructure

We work solely with vetted, US-based enterprise infrastructure providers bound by contractual non-disclosure:

  • Hosting & Storage: Amazon Web Services (AWS, US regions). Customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS.
  • Billing Gateway: Stripe Inc. for encrypted payment transactions.
  • Transactional Communications: Encrypted SMS/Email gateways strictly for dispatching policyholder claim status alerts and adjuster notifications.

Subpoenas & Legal Process: In the event that ClaimHive receives a court order, administrative subpoena, or regulatory demand requesting customer files, we will promptly notify your firm administrator unless prohibited by applicable law, enabling you to assert appropriate work-product privileges or file protective motions.

6. Analytics & Cookies

This public website loads two analytics tools so we can see which pages are useful and whether the site is working. We do not sell this information, and we do not load advertising pixels. These tools run on claimhive.app. They are separate from the claim files inside a firm workspace.

  • Google Analytics 4. We load Google’s gtag.js library. It records page views (the page address and title) and a few actions: viewing the pricing page, and submitting the access request form. A request submission can include the firm name, seat count, cohort, and referral text you entered. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell browsers apart and collects standard technical details that come with that script, such as browser, device, and approximate location. We use it to understand traffic on this site. We do not use it to run ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity. We load Clarity’s script. Clarity records how visitors use public pages — clicks, scrolling, and mouse movement — and turns that into session recordings and heatmaps so we can see where the layout is confusing. The same page-view and form actions above are also sent to Clarity as event names. Clarity sets its own cookies. By default, the Clarity script masks text typed into form fields. We use it to improve the site, not to sell visits.

How to opt out: You can block cookies and scripts in your browser settings. You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You can also use an ad blocker or privacy extension that blocks requests to googletagmanager.com and clarity.ms. Blocking those requests stops both tools on this site.

7. Ownership & 1-Click Full Export

You own your data. If your firm decides to discontinue use of ClaimHive at any time, an authorized administrator can trigger a complete export of all active and closed claim files with a single click. Exports are provided in open, non-proprietary formats (PDF documents, original high-resolution JPEG/PNG inspection images, and CSV chronologies). Upon account closure, all customer data is permanently deleted from production databases and active storage systems after a 30-day recovery grace period.

8. State Insurance Department Compliance Alignment

Our platform controls and data audit mechanisms are structured to support Public Adjuster compliance under state insurance regulatory frameworks, including the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation (OIR), the Texas Department of Insurance (TDI), the Georgia Insurance and Safety Fire Commissioner, and corresponding regulatory bodies across all 50 states.
