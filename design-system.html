# ClaimHive Brand Voice Matrix

DO:
• Speak in Public Adjuster terminology (proof of loss, scopes of work, line items, supplements, appraisal).
• Emphasize reclaimed personal time AND expanded commission revenue.
• Use numbers only when you have the evidence on file, and say whose result it is ("In our own firm, ...").
• Use expansive, unhurried whitespace to counteract their overwhelming workload.

DON'T:
• Don't use generic SaaS tech jargon ("synergy", "paradigm shift", "AI-powered disruption").
• Don't sound patronizing or lecture them on how to estimate damages.
• Don't crowd the screen with dozens of competing visual elements.
• Don't confuse Public Adjusters with Carrier / Independent Adjusters (they work for policyholders!).
