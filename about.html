✦ ABOUT CLAIMHIVE

Built by public adjusters.
For public adjusters.

ClaimHive was designed in the field, not in a boardroom—with input from working public adjusters who know what a claim really takes.

WHERE IT CAME FROM

Built inside a working adjusting firm.

ClaimHive grew directly out of Mission Property Loss Consultants—a working public adjusting firm handling catastrophe and large-loss commercial claims across 25 states and Washington, D.C.

We built the tools we needed to handle more claims without burning out our team—then spent months stress-testing and refining them alongside fellow public adjusters in storm corridors across the country.

The result is software that thinks like an adjuster, anticipates carrier friction, and safeguards statutory deadlines—because adjusters engineered every single screen.

✦ CORE PRINCIPLES

What we stand for.

✦ FOUNDER & ALPHA GENESIS

Forged on the frontline.
Not from a Silicon Valley whiteboard.

ClaimHive was founded by Josh Read, driven by the realization that modern public adjusting firms were drowning in administrative sprawl, disjointed point solutions, and predatory software tolls that took haircuts off policyholder settlements.

Rather than building in isolation, ClaimHive was conceived and engineered inside Mission Property Loss Consultants—a high-volume adjusting firm licensed across 25 states. We built our Alpha engine directly into Mission's daily field operations, stress-testing every automated proof of loss, statute tracker, and Chip AI agent against real carrier pushback and complex commercial files.

The result is a platform honed by adjusters who know the sting of a carrier delay and the exact leverage needed to compel fair, timely claim resolution.
