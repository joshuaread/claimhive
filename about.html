✦ WHO WE ARE

Built by public adjusters.
For public adjusters.

ClaimHive was born out of frustration with legacy enterprise software that treats property insurance claims like generic sales funnels.

THE ORIGIN

Quiet momentum in a noisy industry.

ClaimHive was built inside a working public adjusting firm and refined alongside fellow public adjusters who were tired of spending evenings clicking through 30-tab relational databases.

Public adjusting is high-stakes advocacy. You stand between policyholders whose lives are disrupted and insurance carriers armed with institutional delay tactics. When software gets in your way—forcing manual data entry, hiding statutory deadlines, or demanding third-party add-ons—the policyholder pays the price.

We designed ClaimHive to be the antidote to administrative exhaustion. No flashing alerts. No bloated sales CRM gimmicks. Just quiet, steady file momentum.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Three Uncompromising Values

01

Restraint over Clutter

Software should protect your attention, not compete for it. We reject convoluted modules and endless settings. If a feature doesn't directly eliminate an hour of busywork or strengthen an adjuster's settlement position, it doesn't enter the codebase.

02

File Momentum over Administrative Busywork

A claim file lives and dies by statutory deadlines and procedural pressure. Every tool in ClaimHive—from the 60-day Proof of Loss vault to the bad-faith demand letter engine—is designed to force carrier accountability and keep the settlement clock ticking.

03

Uncompromising Privacy & Data Sovereignty

Your claim files and negotiation notes are legally privileged work product. We harness private, isolated LLMs with an ironclad guarantee: your estimates, photos, and policy files will never be used to train public foundation models. Your data belongs to you.
